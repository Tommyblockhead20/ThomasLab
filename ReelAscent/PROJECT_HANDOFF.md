# REEL ASCENT v17.4

Status: the focused Empire State Building import, centering, grounding, and collision repair is complete as **REEL ASCENT • v17.4**. 2. **Whether the old import was discarded/recreated** — Yes. The runtime GLB was regenerated from the original Blender source after baking the object's original location, rotation, and non-uniform scale. The previous malformed export is replaced; no primitive building approximation, decimation, or face removal was introduced.
3. **Centering method** — The exporter measures the complete transformed mesh bounds, moves the X/Y footprint midpoint to source zero, and leaves a transform-free GLB node. At runtime, PlayCanvas combines every instantiated render mesh AABB and corrects its world X/Z center to the exact Skyreach island structure-root position. This avoids guessed pivots and remains correct if the radial island angle changes.
4. **Grounding method** — The exporter moves the lowest full-mesh vertex to source Z zero. Runtime placement then measures the combined world AABB minimum and aligns it to the island's authored center surface (`location.elevation + 0.18 m`) with a deliberately tiny **0.035 m embed**. The building cannot float, and the embed is too small to visibly bury the facade.
5. **Final footprint and height** — The visible mesh is fitted uniformly in the horizontal plane to approximately **32 × 15.93 m** at its widest source bounds, preserving the supplied footprint ratio instead of stretching its depth to an unrelated 24 m proxy. The main roof remains **381 m / 1,250 ft** above the base, and the highest spire vertex reaches approximately **428.99 m / 1,407.45 ft**.
6. **Detail preservation** — All **2,881 source vertices and 2,868 source polygons** are retained, exporting to **5,736 triangles** across the two original material groups. The real setbacks, narrow upper shaft, crown, spire, facade recesses, and original proportions now come from the supplied mesh; visible boxes do not stand in for the architecture.
7. **Remaining asset limitations** — The supplied 2014 model is intentionally low-poly and has two material groups but no photographic facade textures or interior. It remains a static, non-climbable landmark for this focused baseline. Its 13-box invisible collision hull follows the major footprint/setback changes much more closely than the previous six-box hull, but is still a deliberate gameplay proxy rather than per-triangle collision.
8. **Root cause fixed** — The old GLB stored the original ESB node's non-uniform scale and vertical translation. Runtime `setLocalScale()` and `setLocalPosition()` replaced those node values rather than composing with them, distorting the model and losing its correct offset. Baking and normalizing the source transform removes that conflict; live AABB placement supplies a second deterministic guard against pivot drift.
9. **Island cleanup and collision** — Skyreach still contains only the imported building plus its hidden collision volumes. The v17.2 extras remain absent. Every hull piece is solid, hidden, tagged `non-climbable`, omitted from the climb-surface registry, and overlaps its neighbor so there are no pass-through gaps.
