# Find Your Place metric audit

Reviewed November 22–25, 2026. County and metro views have 44 Live criteria out of 49. City view has 34 Live and 11 Preview criteria. Coverage is measured against **3,221 county records and 393 metro records** in this build, including some historical and territorial records. On-demand criteria are measured after their local data file loads. Some existing scores use proxies and regional fallbacks. The audit checks source definitions, code paths, and coverage, with spot checks against individual source values.

Open coverage gaps include transit frequency across agencies, police reporting, and comparable event calendars.

### On-demand source check

The primary EPA Walkability input covers 3,218 counties and 393 metros. The audited September 2026 transit-stop snapshot covers 689 counties, 131 metros and 6,162 cities. Bike Friendliness previously returned 1,230 county and 380 metro scores. College Access and Intercity Rail each returned 3,221 / 393. Airport Usefulness and the Snowfall preview returned 3,221 / 393 each. Bar Availability covers 3,209 counties and 391 metros. Housing, transportation infrastructure, temperature and other access data load from `metric-data.js`.

The airport join matched all 516 commercial airports with at least 2,500 CY2025 boardings. Completed 2025 passenger flights matched 347 airports in domestic BTS reporting, 71 in the international T-100 record and 349 across both. A regular destination requires at least 36 completed flights across three or more months. Domestic flights group airports into BTS city markets. International flights group airports into destination cities and exclude cargo and charters. The snowfall snapshot contains 5,716 station normals, including 706 genuine zeros. The app still estimates local snowfall from the stations and can assign a warm-climate zero where stations are sparse. The BLS snapshot uses published average weekly wages divided by the U.S. weekly wage of $1,569. The previous location-quotient field was 1.00 for Autauga County, Los Angeles County and New York County despite weekly wages of $1,083, $1,803 and $3,422.

Bike coverage is about **38% of county records**. [FAA final enplanements](https://www.faa.gov/airports/planning_capacity/passenger_allcargo_stats/passenger) · [BTS Aviation Facilities](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/aviation-facilities-33496) · [NOAA Climate Normals](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals) · [BLS quarterly data slices](https://www.bls.gov/cew/additional-resources/open-data/csv-data-slices.htm)

To regenerate the current snapshot, save the 2025 Q4 BLS industry slices listed in `build-find-your-place-data.py` as `bls-<code>.csv`. Save the FAA final commercial enplanement workbook as `faa-final.xlsx` and the NOAA 1991–2020 annual/seasonal v1.0.1 station archive as `noaa-normals.tar.gz`. Run `python scripts/build-find-your-place-data.py <source-folder>`. The script can query BTS coordinates or reuse `airport-points.json`. It can reuse its generated `epa-mobility-counties.json` or query [EPA Smart Location Database v3](https://services.arcgis.com/cJ9YHowT8TU7DUyn/ArcGIS/rest/services/Smart_Location_Database_/FeatureServer/0). For the initial EPA build, install `pyshp` and `shapely` and supply the 2024 Census 1:500,000 county boundary ZIP as `county-boundaries.zip` so the Connecticut block groups match current planning regions.

Save the [official Amtrak timetable](https://content.amtrak.com/content/gtfs/GTFS.zip) as `amtrak-gtfs.zip`. The bundled feed is version 20261123. The builder uses the first 56 service days and takes departure times in each station’s local timezone. Python needs the IANA timezone database. Install `tzdata` if the operating system does not supply it.

To regenerate `metric-data.js`, place these inputs in the same source folder and run `python scripts/build-find-your-place-metrics.py <source-folder>`.

Run `python scripts/build-find-your-place-local-services.py <source-folder>` after refreshing the city directory to rebuild `local-services.js`. In addition to the CMS files below, use the existing HRSA hospital points, 2025 Census county/place/township boundaries, and 2020 block-group population centers. For `cms-hospital-geocoded.csv`, submit public CMS facility ID, street address, city, state and ZIP as headerless rows to the Census Geocoder's address-batch geography endpoint with `Public_AR_Current` and `Current_Current`. The builder uses exact results and checks their county against CMS. A matching HRSA street location is the fallback. [Census Geocoder docs](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html)

Transit also uses `ntd-allocations-2025.csv` from the FTA funding allocation table filtered to `year=2025` and `census-urban-areas-2020.zip` from the Census 1:500,000 cartographic boundaries. A service area passes when operators and mode families with local stops in the September BTS snapshot represent at least 95% of its 2025 reported fixed-route vehicle revenue miles. Outside those urban areas, use the state's non-urban allocation and require observed stop proximity in the county. This checks provider representation and leaves route frequency, operating hours and pedestrian barriers outside the main score. Seattle–Tacoma passed at 96.8%, while Los Angeles–Long Beach–Anaheim fell short at 94.0%. ### National crime data

FBI UCR's 2024 release covered 95.6% of the U.S. population nationally. The final 2025 release covered 96.2%. County scoring still requires agency reporting months and nonoverlapping populations. The FBI/BJS estimates account for missingness at levels such as national, state and city size. The API does not supply a comparable county rate for every county. Older ICPSR county files use allocation and imputation that should not be treated as reported counts.

The practical next step is a **partial county layer with coverage shown**. Join agency reports to jurisdictions and reporting months, resolve overlapping populations, and pool multiple complete years. Compute violent and property rates separately and score only counties meeting explicit reporting and population-coverage thresholds. Validate counties with multiple municipalities and counties spanning reporting systems before expanding it. Keep the current homicide preview independent so its rate is never compared directly with a broader rate.
